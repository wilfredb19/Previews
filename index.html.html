What we do

Our Roofing Services

Specialist roofing across Leeds. Ray and James handle every job personally — no subcontracting, no cutting corners.

🏠
Flat Roof Repairs & Re-felting
Leaking or perished flat roof? Ray's team specialise in flat roof repairs and full re-felting — including loft dormers and bay window roofs. Fixed properly, not patched over.
🌧️
Emergency Leak Repairs
Available 24 hours, Monday to Saturday. If water is getting in, call Ray directly. Fast response, same-day attendance where possible — stopping the damage before it spreads.
🔧
Dry Ridge Systems
Replacing crumbling mortar ridges with a modern dry ridge system — more durable, maintenance-free, and no risk of mortar lifting in bad weather.
🏡
Porch Roofs
New porch roofs and porch roof repairs. Whether it's a full replacement or a repair to an existing structure, we'll give you an honest quote and get it done.
🌿
Guttering
Blocked, leaking or broken gutters cause damp and structural damage fast. We clear, repair and replace guttering across Leeds — keeping rainwater moving where it should.
🔍
Roof Inspections
Not sure what state your roof is in? We'll take a proper look and give you an honest written assessment — no upselling, just straight advice on what needs doing.
