Meet Matt

Three decades on Leeds roofs — and counting.

Welcome. I'm Matt — the bloke behind Matt Boyd Roofing. I've been a roofer for over 30 years, and Matt Boyd Roofing is a family-run business based in Cookridge, Leeds.

I work exclusively with homeowners. No big commercial contracts, no national chains, no call centres. When you ring Matt Boyd Roofing, you get me — the person who'll come and see the job, give you the quote, and do the work.

I cover Leeds and the surrounding areas including Ilkley, Harrogate and Bradford. My business has grown almost entirely through word-of-mouth — customers recommending me to friends and family — which is the reputation I'm proudest of.

Whatever you need — a small repair, a flat roof, or a full re-roof — I'll give you straight advice and a fair quote. No call-out charge. No pressure.

Full Re-Roofing.
Built to last.

If your roof is past repair, a full re-roof is the right move. I strip the old roof, replace any damaged timbers, fit new membrane and battens, and re-tile or re-slate using top-quality materials. You get a new roof, not a patched one, and a comprehensive workmanship guarantee.

✓
Strip & inspect Old roof stripped, structural timbers checked and repaired.
✓
New membrane & battens Modern breathable membrane plus treated battens.
✓
Tiles, slate, lead Re-roof in matching or upgraded materials throughout.
✓
Insulation upgrades Bring loft insulation to current modern standards.

Service Four

Fascias & Soffits.
Crisp roofline, dry walls.

Rotten fascias and soffits don't just look bad — they let damp into your roof structure. I replace tired or rotten boards with new UPVC or timber, fit new bargeboards, and tie everything in with proper guttering. Your house looks ten years younger and you stop the slow damage in its tracks.

✓
UPVC & timber boards Choose modern low-maintenance UPVC or traditional timber.
✓
Bargeboards & cladding Gable end boards and verge cladding to match.
✓
New gutters Fit new guttering and downpipes while we're up there.
✓
Roof ventilation Soffit vents added to prevent loft condensation.

Service Five

Green Roofs.
Beautiful and biodiverse.

A living roof — sedum, wildflower, or grass — turns a flat roof into something genuinely lovely. They look great on garden rooms, extensions and outbuildings, they help with rainwater management, and they're brilliant for biodiversity. I install full systems including waterproof base, drainage, and the planting layer itself.

✓
Sedum systems Low-maintenance sedum mats — drought-tolerant.
✓
Wildflower mixes Bee and pollinator-friendly wildflower planting.
✓
Full waterproof system Root barrier, drainage layer, and growing medium.
✓
Suitable for garden rooms Extensions, outbuildings, sheds, garden offices.
